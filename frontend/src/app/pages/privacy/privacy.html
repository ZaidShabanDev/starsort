★ starsort

Privacy

What Starsort reads, what it keeps, and how to delete it.

What Starsort reads

  • Your public GitHub profile: login, name and avatar.
  • Your public starred repos: names, descriptions, topics and languages.

What it keeps

  • Your starred repos and their categories, so the next run only sends new stars to the AI. Kept until you delete them.
  • Each run's result, deleted after 30 days.
  • Your GitHub ID number and when you last ran Starsort, for the 1-run-per-week limit. This stays after you delete your data, so the limit still works.
  • Server logs, which can include your repo names, deleted after 14 days.

What it never keeps

Your GitHub token. The sign-in token is used once to read your profile, then dropped. The token for syncing Lists is used for one sync, then revoked.

Cookies

One signed sign-in cookie that lasts 8 hours. No ads, tracking or analytics.

AI

Repo names, descriptions and topics (all public) are sent to Claude on Amazon Bedrock in the US. AWS does not share them with Anthropic. AWS may keep them for safety and abuse checks.

Delete your data

  • On your dashboard, click "Delete my data". It deletes your saved stars and runs, and signs you out. Your GitHub Lists and stars are not changed.
  • To remove Starsort's access to your GitHub account, revoke it under GitHub settings → Applications.
